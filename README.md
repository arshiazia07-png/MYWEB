★ MYWEB — Interactive Y2K New Tab Dashboard ★

Transform your default Chrome New Tab into a customizable Y2K-inspired digital canvas and productivity space! MYWEB combines nostalgic retro aesthetics with daily browsing tools, allowing you to decorate your desktop while navigating the web.

✨ Key Features:

. Interactive Canvas Board: Powered by Fabric.js, letting you drag, resize, rotate, and organize objects on your board.

. Full-Body Y2K Avatar Studio: Create and stamp custom characters with hairstyles, Y2K outfits, cat ears, and plushies directly onto your dashboard.

. Y2K Sticker Vault & Doodles: Stamp retro digital stickers (CD-ROMs, tamagotchis, cyber hearts, star doodles, and custom uploaded photos).

. Sticky Notes & Gateway Pad: Keep quick notes, reminders, and a hand-drawn signature directly on your screen.

. Integrated Search Bar: A centralized Google Search engine with quick-access shortcuts to ChatGPT, YouTube, Pinterest, and Instagram.

Built natively for Chrome Manifest V3 with offline local scripts—your board state and layout save directly in your browser.
